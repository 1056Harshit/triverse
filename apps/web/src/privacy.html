Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

TriVerse ("we") runs the TriVerse app and pvtfrnd.com. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Account: your phone number or email, name, and profile photo if you add one.
  • What you share in the app: crop photos, voice messages, chat messages with our assistants and with ride partners, saved places, reminders and settings.
  • Ride drivers: profile photo, vehicle RC and driving licence details, used only to verify you.
  • Location: only when you use a feature that needs it (nearby hospitals, food, rides) and only with your permission.

How we use it

To sign you in, run the features you ask for, keep rides safe, prevent fraud and send you sign-in codes and important account emails. We do not sell your data.

Who processes it for us

Supabase (database and file storage), Render (servers), Google Gemini (AI answers), 2Factor (SMS and call codes) and Google (email). They handle data only to provide their service to us. AI answers are generated from the text, photos or voice you send.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. When you delete your account we delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep records.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent and raise a complaint. Delete your account any time in Settings → Account → Delete account or see Delete account.
